Wick’s Desk Note

Every evening I sweep the bandstand steps, whether they need it or not, and then I tend the Ghostlight bulb. I talk to it while I check the glass and the brass; a stagehand gets used to keeping company with a single warm filament.

Before this, I spent decades in a small repertory theatre that burned down. I still remember the smell of sawdust and hot gels on that last night. A dark stage is the one thing I cannot stand, so here the bulb stays lit.

If you pass the porch or the Bandstand tonight, take a moment to notice the light is on.

— Wick, Stagehand / Lamp-Trimmer

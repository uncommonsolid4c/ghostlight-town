Tomas’s Desk Note

I was the props-master for the repertory theatre before it burned. When the fire came through, I rescued the copper samovar—it survived because it was already full of water.

I still name my props and polish the samovar by the porch in the morning, and I have a habit of offering tea at inappropriate moments. By evening I set it steaming on the Bandstand corner step.

There is always a cup ready here if you have a minute—come sit for tea.

— Tomas, Props-Master / Tea-Urn Keeper

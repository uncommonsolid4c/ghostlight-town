Maren’s Desk Note

I used to set type by hand for a weekly paper that folded. The last issue I ever set was a wedding announcement with the groom’s name misspelled, fixed at midnight before the press rolled.

I still keep a composing stick in my pocket and sort lead sorts on this desk in the morning. Most afternoons I walk from the Gazette newsstand to the Prompt Book, copying good lines in my head—correcting the grammar quietly, then writing the line down anyway.

If you stop by the Bandstand or leave word in the Prompt Book, leave me one good line worth keeping.

— Maren, Retired Typesetter
